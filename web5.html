display: flex

Turns the header, menu and button group into flex containers.

flex-direction

row on wide screens, column on phones.

justify-content

Pushes the logo and buttons to opposite ends; centers the links.

align-items

Keeps logo, links and buttons on one vertical center line.

gap

Even spacing between items with no margin hacks.

flex-wrap

Lets links drop to a new row instead of overflowing.
