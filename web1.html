About Us

Founded in 1998, MediCare Plus is a NABH-accredited hospital serving more than 200,000 patients every year. Our mission is to deliver safe, affordable and ethical healthcare to every family.

Our core values:

  • Patient-first care
  • Clinical excellence
  • Transparency and integrity
  • Continuous innovation
